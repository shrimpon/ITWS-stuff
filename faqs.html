Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about solar energy and solar panel systems.

What is solar energy?

Solar energy is electricity or heat obtained from sunlight using solar technologies.

How do solar panels work?

Solar panels convert sunlight into electrical energy that can be used to power appliances and other electrical equipment.

Can solar panels work during cloudy days?

Yes. Solar panels can still generate electricity when sunlight is limited, although their output is generally reduced.

What is a solar battery?

A solar battery stores electricity generated by a solar system for later use.
